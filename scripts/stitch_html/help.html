Frequently Asked

You can track your order directly from the "Orders" tab in your profile. Once an order is dispatched, you'll see real-time updates and an estimated delivery window.

Refunds can be initiated within 7 days of delivery. Go to your Orders, select the specific item, and choose "Request Refund". Our team usually processes these within 24-48 hours.

You can modify the delivery address before the order is marked as 'Dispatched'. Head to the order details page and click 'Edit Address'.
